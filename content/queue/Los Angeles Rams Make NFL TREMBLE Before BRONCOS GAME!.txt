But here's where the conversation gets genuinely fun — because the loudest storyline in the national media this week is an apology tour. And it's aimed at Davante Adams.

A red-zone specialist. A great career winding down gracefully. That was the league-wide assumption: future Hall of Famer, sure — first ballot, probably — but past the part of his career where he takes over football games. And the conclusion they landed on is the one that should terrify the NFC: the Rams don't have a number one receiver and a fading star. They've got two number one receivers, period.
