Now let me tell you what limited actually means, because it’s the single most misread word in football coverage.

The league requires teams to file a participation report on practice days. There are three categories, and only three. Did not participate. Limited. Full.

Limited means the player did some portion of practice and not all of it. That’s the entire content of the word. It does not describe an injury, a severity, a prognosis or a probability.

And here’s why it’s so slippery. Limited covers two completely different situations that are indistinguishable from the outside.

A player can be limited because he’s genuinely compromised and the team is protecting him from contact. Or a player can be limited because he’s fine, it’s Thursday, and there is no reason on earth to give a veteran a full workload three days before a game when his hamstring is a little tight.

Both of those produce the identical line on the identical form. So limited is a participation label, not a medical one, and the video is dead right to say there’s no need to read more into it than the source supports.
